MAPTOBER 2026 • SCORING & BONUS SYSTEM

Fair weighting for 3–4 Jury members, Size Bonus & Homerun Bonus

1. Score Formula per Mapart

With 3 to 4 jury members voting this year, the base score is calculated as the average (Ø 1.00 to 10.00 points) of all jury votes:

Map Score = Ø Jury Score (1–10 pts) + Size Bonus (+1 to +10 pts)
2. Size Bonus Brackets (Max. +10 Points)
Tier Area (Maps) Common Sizes Bonus
Mini / Standard 1 Map 1×1 (incl. 3D/Staircased) +1 pt
Small 2 – 4 Maps 1×2, 2×1, 3×1, 2×2 +3 pts
Medium 5 – 9 Maps 2×3, 3×2, 5×1, 4×2, 3×3 +5 pts
Large 10 – 20 Maps 3×4, 5×3, 6×3, 4×5 +8 pts
Mega Mapart 21+ Maps 7×3, 5×5, 8×8, 9×6, 16×10 +10 pts
3. Homerun Bonus (All 31 Days Completed) +35 POINTS

Artists or teams completing a valid mapart for every single day from Oct 1 to Oct 31 (31/31 days) receive a one-time Homerun Bonus of +35 points.
